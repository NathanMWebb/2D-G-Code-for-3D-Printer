Tips for best results
  • High contrast or line art images trace best. Photos need contrast boosted first.
  • Increase contrast slider before adjusting threshold — it cleans up faint lines.
  • Bump up Pre-blur for noisy or textured images (like pencil sketches).
  • Tape paper flat to the build plate before every run.
  • Start pen-down Z at -0.5mm and dial in from there.
  • Drag the .gcode file into Bambu Studio to load and send to printer.
  • Disable filament detection before running any pen plotter G-code.
